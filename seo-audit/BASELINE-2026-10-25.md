### 4.1 Two patterns repeat across every competitor and are absent here

I sampled the actual pages rather than counting URLs, because the counts
mislead in both directions.

**Pattern 1 — a template library.** AiSensy's 74 `/whatsapp-chatbot-template/*`
pages are **1,863 and 2,130 words** in my two samples, each with per-template
substance plus a repeated conversion scaffold. This is the pattern worth
emulating, and TopEdge has the raw material: Meta-approved templates are a real
shipped capability.

**Counter-example, do not copy:** DelightChat's 4,936 `/apps/*` pages are
**523–526 words**, H1 = app name, a single H2 = developer name. That is
mass-produced thin content. It inflates their URL count by 69% and it is exactly
what the brief forbids. **Wati's 111 `/whatsapp-template` URLs are also not what
they look like** — two different URLs
(`whatsapp-templates-festival/`, `easter-holiday-whatsapp-templates/`) both
serve the *same* 4,744-word page with an identical title and H1, so that family
is largely consolidated onto one pillar. Raw competitor counts overstate the
asset; I checked.

**Pattern 2 — case studies as their own URLs.** Interakt 71, AiSensy 65,
Gallabox 62, LimeChat 47, DoubleTick 34, Zoko 21. **TopEdge has zero** — one
`/customers` page at 705 words carrying all of it. Interakt's `/case-study/` hub
is only 860 words but fronts 7+ individually named brands.

We have three named merchants with live stores and attributed quotes already on
the site (§3.2). That is enough for three real case studies today, with no
invention required.

## 5. Where this connects to the paid account

From project memory, verified live 11–18 Oct: C5 "Platform" is effectively the
whole Google Ads account (365 impressions, 28 clicks, 7.67% CTR, ₹12.59 CPC),
and within C7 the best CTR in the account — 14.29% — belongs to ad group AG13,
**"Shopify apps"**. Keyword Planner put `shopify apps` at 1k–10k/month in India
with **Low** competition and a ₹1.35–₹84.75 range.

Meanwhile the COD/RTO keyword thesis was killed on evidence: `cod confirmation
app shopify india`, `confirm cod orders on whatsapp`, `reduce rto shopify` and
`whatsapp cart recovery app` all return **no data** in Keyword Planner.

**Hypothesis (not yet tested organically):** the organic equivalent of C5/AG13 is
`/shopify-whatsapp-integration` and `/integrations` — the Shopify-ecosystem
pages. They are currently **474 and 541 words, carry zero rupee figures, and
have the two weakest internal link profiles on the site** (17 and 13 inbound).
The one place where paid has demonstrated demand is the place organic has
invested least. Note DelightChat's third-largest family is `/best-shopify-apps`
(334 URLs) — they are working this seam, if thinly.

I am flagging this as a hypothesis rather than a finding because paid
performance does not prove organic performance for the same term, and because
without GSC I cannot see whether these pages already get impressions.
