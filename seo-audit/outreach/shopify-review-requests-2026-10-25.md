  review including what is weak.
- No invented contact details. **You supply the contacts** — these are your
  customers, and I will not guess at phone numbers or emails.
- No claim that a review has been obtained until the review is live on the
  listing. Track status in `BACKLINK_PROSPECTS`-style columns at the bottom.
