The hidden cost

A returned COD order doesn't cost you a sale. It costs you three payments.

Every parcel a buyer refuses at the door is shipping fees out, shipping fees back, and packaging, paid before you've made a single rupee.

Shipping outThe courier is paid to carry the parcel to the buyer, whether they accept it or not.
Shipping backRefused at the door? You pay the courier again to bring it back.
Packaging & handlingPlus the packing materials, warehouse time, and restocking, for a sale that never happened.

TopEdge removes the guesswork. Buyers confirm on WhatsApp before you ship, so every parcel that leaves your warehouse is one the buyer actually wants.

Three steps. Zero code. Live in about 15 minutes.

  1. A COD order is placedShopify tells TopEdge the instant the order is created.
  2. The buyer gets a WhatsApp messageA Meta-approved template. They tap Confirm or Cancel.
  3. You ship what they confirmNo-reply orders get followed up automatically or held, your call.
