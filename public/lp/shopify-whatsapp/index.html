Before you hand over your Meta account

Will my WhatsApp number get banned?

Messages send on the official WhatsApp Cloud API, not an unofficial bridge, which is where bans come from. Every template is approved by Meta on your own Business account before it can send, so nothing goes out that Meta has not already cleared.

What does Meta actually charge me?

Meta bills your own Business account directly, at its own per-conversation rates, and TopEdge adds 0% on top. You pay us for the software and Meta for the messages, and you can see both bills separately.

Do I need my own Meta Business account, or do you give me a number?

Your own. Your number, your templates, your quality rating. If you ever leave, you keep all three; there is nothing to migrate back off us.

How long does setup take?

Installing from the Shopify App Store and switching on a journey takes about fifteen minutes. The part that is not in your hands is Meta template approval, which is usually same day but is Meta’s clock, not ours.

I am already on another WhatsApp tool. Can I move my flows?

Yes. Send us what you have running and we rebuild it here before you cancel anything, so you are never between two tools. There is no import button for this; a person does it with you.

What happens if I cross my order limit?

Nothing switches off mid-month and no message is silently dropped. You get a notice as you approach the limit and the option to move up a plan; the difference is prorated against what you have already paid.

Do I get a GST invoice?

Automatically, every cycle, with your GSTIN on it. Prices on this page are before 18% GST.
