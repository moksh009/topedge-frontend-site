Before you start

How much does it cost?

From ₹1,999 a month + 18% GST, or ₹1,599 billed yearly. COD confirmation is on every plan. Meta bills its own per-message fees to your account and TopEdge adds 0% markup.

What if the customer does not reply?

You decide: auto follow-up, or hold the order for review. Nothing ships until you have set that rule.

Do templates need Meta approval?

Yes, and TopEdge will not send one that is not approved yet. That is what protects your number.

Which plan has COD to prepaid?

Growth and Scale. It sends a payment link built from a Shopify draft invoice, so the buyer can pay upfront instead. Launch covers basic COD confirmation.

Will it work with my checkout?

Yes. It connects through Shopify order webhooks. No theme edits, no checkout scripts, no developer.

Is my WhatsApp number safe?

Yes. Messages go through the official WhatsApp Cloud API on approved templates. It is not a workaround.
